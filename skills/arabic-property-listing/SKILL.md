# Arabic property listing

Property buyers and tenants scan for the same facts first: location, size, price, layout, handover or availability, and payment terms. Lead with those, then sell the lifestyle.

## Gather (never invent any of these)

- Type, community and building, size (sq ft and m²), bedrooms and bathrooms, floor and view.
- Price or rent, service charges if known, payment plan for off-plan.
- Status: ready, vacant, tenanted, off-plan with expected handover.
- Real amenities and distances (minutes to metro, school, mall), measured or from the developer.
- **The advertising permit number your regulator requires** (for example a Trakheesi permit in Dubai) and the broker or company license number. Ads without the required permit can be removed or fined, so ask for it and leave a clear `[permit number]` placeholder if missing.
- Who the listing is for: investor, family, first-time buyer.

## Outputs

1. **Portal listing**: English title (≤ 70 characters), Arabic title, then a bilingual description: a 2-line summary, key facts as a list, features, location and distances, terms, permit and contact.
2. **Reel script (30 s)**: hook with the most surprising true fact (price per sq ft, view, payment plan), a walk-through in shot order (entrance, living, kitchen, bedrooms, view, amenities), one CTA.
3. **WhatsApp message**: 5 to 7 short lines with the facts, permit number and one question to continue the conversation.

## Rules

- Accurate, specific, no exaggeration: avoid «أفضل استثمار» or guaranteed returns. Do not promise rental yields or price growth.
- Prices with currency and digits 0-9 (AED 1,250,000). Use the `arabic-tafqeet` package if the amount must also appear in words.
- English names of communities and developers in English letters.
- No dashes or slashes in titles.
- Fair housing: describe the property, not who should or should not live there.
