# Arabic car review

Car buyers in the region watch reviews to answer a few practical questions: price here, range or fuel use in our heat, space, tech that works in Arabic, service and resale. Answer those with real experience.

## Gather first

- Exact model, trim and model year, and whether it was owned, borrowed or a test drive.
- The creator's own experience: days driven, kilometers, real range or fuel use, charging or fuel costs, problems.
- Local price and where it came from (dealer, official site, date). Never guess a price; if unknown, say «تواصل مع الوكيل» or mark `[verify price]`.
- Specs from the official manufacturer or regional distributor site. Cite the source for every number.
- Format: Reel (45–60 s), or long YouTube (8–15 min).

## Structure (Reel)

1. **Hook (0–3 s)**: the surprising result or the question owners ask. Use a real number from the creator's use.
2. **Who it's for (3–8 s)**.
3. **3 real points (8–45 s)**: one strength, one weakness, one thing nobody mentions. Each with a shot of that exact feature.
4. **Verdict (45–55 s)**: buy, wait, or consider X instead, and for whom.
5. **CTA**: one action.

For long videos add: walk-around, interior and space test, tech and Arabic support, driving and comfort, real range or fuel test in summer heat, ownership costs, verdict with alternatives.

## Rules

- When a model or feature is named, the shot shows that exact thing.
- Comparisons are fair: same conditions, same trims where possible, and the source for each number.
- Do not attack a brand or dealer; describe your experience and facts.
- Disclose sponsorship or a loaned car clearly at the start and in the caption.
- English model names in English letters (BYD Seal, Tesla Model Y); digits 0-9.
- No dashes or slashes in the title.

## Output

A beat table (time, spoken line in the creator's dialect, on-screen text, shot list), a list of every number used with its source, a title, a cover text of 2 to 4 words, and a caption.
