# Arabic comment replies

Replies keep a post alive and turn viewers into followers and clients. Answer the comments that matter first, in the creator's voice.

## Sort first

1. **Leads**: price, availability, "كيف بتواصل معك؟", collaboration. Reply fast; move details to DM or WhatsApp; never post private details publicly.
2. **Real questions**: answer in one or two lines; if the answer is long, say a video is coming and note it as a content idea.
3. **Critics with a point**: thank, agree with what is right, correct facts calmly with a source.
4. **Trolls and insults**: do not argue. A short neutral reply or no reply; hide or report abuse.
5. **Fans**: short, personal, varied (not the same emoji on 50 comments).

## Rules

- Write in the commenter's dialect when you can, otherwise the creator's.
- One to two sentences. Ask a question back when it keeps a good conversation going.
- No promises the creator cannot keep (prices, availability, results).
- Never share personal data or argue about religion or politics.
- English names in English letters, digits 0-9.

## Output

A table: comment, category, suggested reply, and "→ video idea" when a question deserves its own content. End with the 3 best content ideas found in the comments.
