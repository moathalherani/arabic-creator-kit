# Arabic content calendar

A calendar the creator can actually keep beats an ambitious one they abandon in week two.

## Gather

- Niche and 3 to 4 content pillars (for example: cars, AI tools, real estate, behind the scenes).
- Platforms and realistic output per week (how many Reels can they film and edit?).
- Recurring formats that already work for them (ask for their top 3 posts).
- Market (UAE, KSA, Jordan, Egypt…) for local dates.
- Fixed commitments: brand deals, launches, travel.

## Regional dates to check

- Islamic occasions move every year. If the `arabic-dates` package is available, get planning dates with `islamicOccasions(year)`: Ramadan, Eid al-Fitr, Arafah, Eid al-Adha, Hijri New Year, Mawlid. Note that official dates follow moon sighting.
- Fixed national days by market (UAE 2 December, Saudi 23 September, Jordan 25 May, Egypt 23 July, Qatar 18 December, Kuwait 25 February…). Confirm the ones you include.
- Seasons: summer heat, back-to-school (late August/September), White Friday and year-end sales, GITEX and other big local events.
- During Ramadan, viewing peaks shift late at night; plan posting times accordingly.

## Build

1. **Pillar mix**: about 40% the main pillar, 30% second, 20% third, 10% personal or behind the scenes.
2. **Weekly rhythm**: one "big" piece (YouTube or a deeply researched Reel), 2 to 4 short Reels, 1 carousel, daily stories, 2 to 3 LinkedIn posts if relevant.
3. **Batching**: film on 1 or 2 days, edit on another; leave one buffer slot per week.
4. **Repurpose**: each big piece becomes 2 Reels, 1 carousel and 1 LinkedIn post.

## Output

A table: date, day, platform, format, pillar, working title (no dashes or slashes), hook idea, status. Then a short list of what to film this week, and the 3 dates that need content prepared in advance.
