# Arabic brand pitch

Brand managers get many generic pitches. A good one is short, shows you know their product, proposes one concrete idea, and proves it with your real numbers.

## Gather

- Brand, product, and the market (UAE, KSA, GCC, Levant, Egypt).
- Why this brand fits: a real reason (you use it, your audience asks about it, a past video on the topic).
- The creator's real numbers: followers per platform, average views per Reel or video over the last 30 to 90 days, audience top countries, a best-performing related post with its link. Never round up or invent; if unknown, leave `[number]`.
- What the creator offers: Reel, story set, YouTube integration, UGC, event, and rough rate or "rates on request".
- Language of the brand contact (Arabic, English, or both).

## Email structure (≤ 150 words)

1. **Subject**: specific, under 60 characters, with the brand and the idea ("Reel idea: BYD Seal summer range test").
2. **Line 1**: who you are in one sentence, with one proof number.
3. **Line 2 and 3**: the idea, in one or two sentences, and why it fits their goal right now (launch, season, Ramadan, summer).
4. **Proof**: 2 or 3 bullet numbers and one link.
5. **Ask**: one easy next step (a 15-minute call, the media kit, a rate card).
6. **Sign-off**: name, role, one email, one phone or WhatsApp, website.

## Follow-ups

- Day 4 to 5: one line adding something new (a fresh result, a date).
- Day 10 to 12: a polite close ("happy to revisit later").

## Rules

- No copy-paste flattery or long bios. No attachments in the first email unless asked; link the media kit.
- Be clear about paid vs. gifted collaboration; disclose sponsored content in posts as local rules require (for example the UAE's advertiser permit rules for influencers).
- Never claim results you have not had.
- Provide both Arabic and English versions when the brand is regional and the contact's language is unknown.

## Output

Subject, email, two follow-ups, and a one-paragraph media kit summary the creator can reuse.
